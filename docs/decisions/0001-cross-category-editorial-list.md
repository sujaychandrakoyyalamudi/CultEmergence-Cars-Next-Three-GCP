# ADR 0001: Use a cross-category editorial collection

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-26

## Decision

Present ten category winners in an editorial reading order instead of computing a universal score across unrelated vehicle missions.

## Why

A single formula that ranks a family hybrid against a sports car, luxury SUV and performance EV would imply precision the evidence does not support. Category framing makes value judgments explicit and permits honest trade-offs.

## Consequences

The methodology must remain visible. Rank numbers are editorial navigation, not a scientific score. Replacing a vehicle requires preserving brand/mission diversity.
